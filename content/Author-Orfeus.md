Spineshatter (A)

I have always enjoyed quite many aspects of the game, like discovering secret locations (got banned for making it to GM Island), the community, raiding, lore, roleplaying, but mostly theorycrafting. 

I tend to question things presented as meta with no data to back it up, I have noticed each server has different metas sometimes and when you check the sources, they just present some formulas, black-box simulators and rules without derivations. This sparks my curiosity. Hence I made this hobby archive to share my findings.

Majority of my time I have played on Excalibur TBC server (R.I.P.) As a rookie I joined Sunwell progression guild, which later became what is now called a hardcore guild. This term didn't exist back then, I think the people on the server rather called us as a toxic guild. We were friendly, but not towards applicants who had no clue what is going on, nor other guilds who performed worse. I feel shameful when writing this, because I was such an applicant once. 

Later, when friends left the game, I became the master of the guild, stepped down after few months and left for another guild, it was lead by a girl from the oldschool Paragon guild and I think the best people of that era had met there, big plus was we had a great chemistry, we knew of each other before the project started, everyone was raiding in different guilds tho. GM leaving the guild is not ideal, I get it, but by that time I had more friends outside of the guild, than in the guild, and I could feel people were friendly towards me, just because they wanted raid spots and loot, it was annoying and I wanted to be a simple raider again. So I went where my friends were.

The records are now pretty much irrelevant, since so many pro-players, with experience from harder content, have joined the game. But we had very laid back atmosphere, with healthy amount of slack and still cleared SWP in an hour, wiping was extremely rare and we had quite a lot of fun in vocal. I have seen current 30 min SWP runs, it is just a rush, admirable ofcourse. The difference between 1h run and 30 min is insane. Also the records are non-existent now, because realmplayers and similar projects, on which were logs from all the TBC private servers, do not exist anymore. 

I have tried telling girls on dates I was the #1 mage and #2 CoH on realmplayers, but it didn't do anything with them for some reason. So I have started a business instead and now after 8 years I thought I might try it again.

What I think many guilds forget is, that even if you wipe for hours, but have fun doing it, you call it a great evening. Everyone cares mostly about logs, pars, some datasheets to satisfy their egos. Let me tell you being #1 doesn't make the game any better, or more enjoyable, it is only the people you spend your time with, it is pretty much the same like in the real life. 

I was known as Alodia, my main was mage and CoH priest, hence on this archive I talk mostly about the casting class mechanics.
