Back2Basics - TBC

I have always enjoyed quite many aspects of the game, like discovering secret locations (got banned to making it to GM Island), the community, raiding, but mostly theorycrafting. 

I have always questioned things presented as meta with no data to back it up, also I enjoy mathematics so theorycrafting is sort of fun for me.

Majority of my time I have played on Excalibur TBC server (RIP), I was mostly known as Alodia, there I have met wonderful people I am stil in contact with after almost 10 years. As a rookie I joined Sunwell progression guild, which later became what is now called a hardcore guild. This term didn't exist back then, I think the people on the server rather considered us as a toxic guild. We were friendly, but not towards applicants who had no clue what is going on. I feel shameful sometimes, because I was such an applicant once. Later I became GM of the guild, which I didn't enjoy, hence I left for another, where we managed some sunwell runs under 1h (record by that time afaik), or I have outdpsed M'uru few times on my mage. Essentially I have made mages superior to warlocs in tbc on most fights. It still warms my little ego.

Then I figured in my early 20's that I have spent around 10% of my time lived in the game, hence I deleted the game completely and started a photography business. 

Now after almost 10 years I found myself missing the game, hence I am back. Still not sure if I want to go the PvE path again, maybe rather just focus on what I like the most.

I have accomplished few things in the game already, but there is still so much to learn and do, like PvP, roleplaying, or market control.
