## Introduction

Damage dealers, supportive damage dealers, healers and tanks have different roles in the WoW game. One way to categorize them is via their roles on a competetive - supportive spectrum.

## Purely supportive roles

Here I categorize tanks and holy paladins. 

Tanks do not compete with anyone, their role is to make raid as smooth as possible for all other classes via correct positioning, usage of cooldowns, threat management and gear. They are the most important raid members because they are usually the only ones who can do their task.

Holy paladins have a special role among healers in TBC and that is to keep tank alive. It is their foremost responsibility and hence they should not compete with other healers in healing done. They might just stand for seconds precasting and cancelling Holy Light on a tank, essentially doing nothing competetive. 

## Mixed healing roles

Here is the rest of the healing classes (resto druids, holy priests, resto shamans)

The healers role here is again keeping the tanks / raid alive, but they all compete for the same health pool. The competetivness in healing has many negative effects, because sometimes you have a decision - heal someone, who is very low with a quick cast vs. AoE raid heal which will push your healing done up. So a healer should have some ethic about this and not care too much for the meters. 

People are naturally competetive and it will always be the case for theese classes that they compare performances. Hence it is important to set the mood such, that they instead tend to cooperate, don't praise healing done, but situations and decisions made.

## Mixed damage roles

Here are supportive damage classes (shadow priests, enhancement and elemental shamans, balance druids, arms warriors, survival hunters, retribution paladins)

Their goal is not necesarrily to be top dps, but to do the assigned job to help purely competetive classes. If a shaman struggles to keep totem rotation up, he fails his job. If he looses in dps to the other shaman, it just should motivate him to ask how he managed. Maybe it is just an issue of latency and not the player's skill. Also remember it is not only the damage the class does alone, but also the effect of buffing the other classes compensating for his lower dps output, this effect should be calculated and added to meters to make it fair.

## Purely competetive roles

Rest of the damage classes belong here (Mages, Warlocks, BM Hunters, Fury Warriors, Rogues)

Except few encounters (like a mage or warlock tank, then they are not in this role) their role is to beat each other in the damage meters and not wipe the raid while doing it. (Stop DPS means stop DPS, not top DPS). 

The most unfortunate in this group are PvE rogues, who have lowest dps and no raid utility. But I think being a fun person and keeping the raid spirits up, or doing raidleading is much more essential than any other in-game buff, hence such a rogue with social / leadership skills could be considered to be in the mixed role and have an essential spot in raids.
