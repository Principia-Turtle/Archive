## Prior reading

The effect of spell crit was used as an example in [[Mechanics of Random Number Based Game.md]], so read that first.

The only thing worth to add is that each spell can have different crit coefficients based on talents, usually it is 200%. So having 1% of crit is the same like having 1% more haste for spells with 2x coefficients.

22.1 Spell Crit Rating increases critical strike chance by 1%.
